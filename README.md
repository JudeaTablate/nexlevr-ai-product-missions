# Nexlevr AI Product Missions

A portfolio project exploring AI product management through product analysis, feature planning, prototyping, user research, and roadmap development.

**Live Portfolio:** https://ai-product-missions.hatchable.site/?_hbpvclear=1

**Created by:** Judea Ann C. Tablate

## About the Project

This portfolio brings together a series of AI product missions designed to practice the work involved in taking a product idea from discovery to planning and validation.

The project focuses on understanding user problems, evaluating product opportunities, translating ideas into requirements, and making decisions based on user feedback and product goals.

## Product Missions

### Beginner — Product Teardown

A teardown of ChatGPT for Business, looking at its product experience and identifying opportunities for improvement.

- Product and feature analysis
- User pain points
- Feature ideation
- Product improvement recommendations

### Intermediate — PRD and Prototype

A product concept for an AI-powered booking assistant for Studio Bloom.

- Product Requirements Document (PRD)
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- AI behavior and guardrails
- Interactive booking assistant prototype
- User testing plan and success metrics

### Advanced — Discovery and Validation

A deeper product planning exercise focused on identifying opportunities and evaluating whether a proposed solution is worth pursuing.

- Discovery hypotheses
- User interview plan
- Opportunity tree
- Prototype and usability testing plan
- Go/no-go recommendation

### Six-Month Product Roadmap

A directional roadmap outlining potential product initiatives over six months.

- Feature prioritization
- Opportunity and impact assessment
- Sequencing of product improvements
- Rationale behind roadmap decisions

## What I Learned

Through these missions, I explored how product managers connect user needs with product decisions. The work helped me practice breaking down ambiguous problems, writing clearer requirements, considering usability and AI limitations, and thinking about how a product should be tested before launch.

## Notes on Research and Validation

The portfolio includes proposed research methods, interview plans, and testing plans. Any research or testing results should be treated as completed only when actual participant feedback has been collected and documented.

The roadmap is a portfolio exercise and should not be interpreted as an official Nexlevr product roadmap.
